About CampusFest

Connecting students, organizers, and campus life through seamless event discovery and management.

Our Mission

Empowering campus engagement through technology. We believe that a vibrant campus life is central to the student experience. CampusFest is built to tear down the logistical barriers between students and the events that enrich their time at university, creating a more connected and active community.

Key Features

Event Discovery

Easily browse and search for upcoming campus events based on categories, dates, and interests. Find exactly what you're looking for, instantly.

Easy Registration

Secure your spot with one-click registration. Manage your upcoming schedule and past attended events all from a single, organized dashboard.

Real-time Notifications

Never miss an update. Receive instant alerts for schedule changes, important announcements, and approaching event start times directly to your device.

How it Works

Your journey to a more engaging campus life in three simple steps.

1

Create Profile

Sign up with your university email to access the exclusive student portal.

2

Browse & RSVP

Explore the event feed, find activities that match your interests, and click register.

3

Attend & Engage

Show up, scan your digital ticket if required, and enjoy your campus experience.
